Placement FAQs

Questions to consider when you are looking for a facility.

Who can reach out?

Family members, caregivers, and care professionals can start with a general inquiry.

What should I ask a facility?

Ask about availability, admission requirements, costs, and how the facility works with your hospice provider.

Who confirms admission?

The facility confirms availability and admission. Your hospice team can help clarify care needs and coordinate next steps.

What if our timeline is short?

Share your general timeframe and stay in touch with your hospice team or discharge planner about immediate needs.

How do I find out what it will cost?

Ask the facility for a written breakdown and check coverage with your insurer or benefits contact.

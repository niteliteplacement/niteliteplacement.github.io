The placement process

How placement works

A conversation can help you sort through facility options and prepare for the next step.

01

Share your priorities

Start with your preferred area, general timeline, and what matters most to your family.

02

Explore options

Discuss facility settings and prepare questions about openings, costs, and coordination with your hospice team.

03

Plan the transition

Confirm admission and move details with the facility and your hospice team.

Before choosing a facility

Ask who will coordinate with your hospice team, what is included in the cost, and what needs to happen before moving day. Confirm admission directly with the facility.
